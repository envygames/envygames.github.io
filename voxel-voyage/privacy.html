Voxel Voyage privacy policy

ENVY Games · last updated 1 October 2026

Voxel Voyage is a game for Android made by ENVY Games, the independent studio of Neil Dsouza ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, and why. In short: we don't run any servers and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. The game uses Google's services for ads and purchases, and those services handle some information as described below.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your progress: scores, worlds found, cubes and extras unlocked, shards, levels and settings.
  • The year you were born, which the game asks once so it can choose suitable ads (see "Children" below). It is never sent anywhere by the game.

Android may back up this game data to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and a few short ads between runs (none in your first runs, never right after a very short run, and at least three minutes apart). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • If you are in a region where the law requires consent (such as the European Economic Area or the UK), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you can change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Ads for players under 18 are never personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (No Ads, shard packs, animated cubes) are made through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game what you bought so it can give it to you, and so No Ads can be restored on a new phone. The purchasing library (Unity In-App Purchasing) may send Unity technical information about the purchase, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

Reminders (optional)

If you allow notifications, the game sets at most one reminder a day on your phone (for example, when new daily goals are ready). Reminders are created on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off any time in Android's settings for the app.

Children

Voxel Voyage is for everyone. The game asks the year you were born once (a neutral question, with no answer that "unlocks" anything). For players under 13, ads are child-directed: only from Google's Families-certified ad network, never personalised, and limited to content suitable for all ages. The game has no chat, no profiles and no way to share personal information with other players. Sharing a daily run result uses your phone's own share menu, and only sends what you choose to send.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent (where offered): Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Stop all ads between runs: the one-time No Ads purchase.
  • Delete the game's data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android's app settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: envyagamestudio@gmail.com.

If you write to us (Settings → Feedback opens an email for you), we see what you send, including the game's version and your phone's model and Android version, which the game adds to the email so we can look into problems. We use it only to answer you and fix the game.
