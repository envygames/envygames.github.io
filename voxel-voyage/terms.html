Your account and cloud save

Players 13 and over can sign in with Google Play Games to keep their progress in a cloud save and carry it to another phone. Keep your Google account secure: whoever can use it can play your save. We look after the cloud save with care, but we can't promise it will never be lost, and progress has no cash value. You can turn the cloud save off or delete it, with your player, at any time in Settings → MANAGE.

The daily board

Players 13 and over can join the daily board, which ranks the day's track in groups of 50. To keep it fair:

  • Only scores from genuine runs in an unmodified copy of the game count. Scores that couldn't have come from a real run are refused, and we may remove scores or players we believe cheated.
  • Board names are made up by the game; you can't choose one, so nothing personal appears there.
  • Each day has its own board. Old boards are cleared within 3 days, and boards may change, pause or be withdrawn.
  • You can leave the board at any time from the Daily page. If you aren't signed in, that deletes your board player and scores; if you are, nothing more is sent and your scores go with the board's reset, and DELETE MY DATA (Settings → MANAGE) deletes your player at once.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and a few short ads between runs. Buying No Ads ends the ads between runs; the ads you choose to watch stay available.

Children

The game asks for your year of birth once. Players under 13 get ads suitable for children, never personalised, and the daily board isn't offered to them. Parents can find more in the privacy policy.
